# Google reviews widget

The left half of the reviews section embeds the owner-provided Elfsight Google
Reviews widget. Justdial and IndiaMART are stacked in the equally wide right half.
The widget app ID and loader URL are in `src/lib/site-data.ts`.

In the Elfsight dashboard, open this widget, choose the Slider layout, enable
Autoplay, and publish. Review sources, presentation and automatic sliding are
controlled by Elfsight. The Google Maps iframe is not used for reviews.

The free Elfsight plan includes its branding badge.
